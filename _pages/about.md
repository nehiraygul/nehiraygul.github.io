I am a PhD student and a scientific researcher at the Institute of Linguistics at Goethe University in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. I began my PhD as a part of the research training group [Nominal Modification](https://www.fb10.uni-frankfurt.de/50692667), and I am currently working in colloboration with the B01 project at the SFB 1629 [NegLaB](https://www.neglab.de/projects/b01/). My research interests primarily lie in the area of psycholinguistics (interpretation and production) and its interfaces with syntax and semantics. I find scope, binding and anaphora particularly interesting and am currently working on quantifier scope in German and Turkish.
